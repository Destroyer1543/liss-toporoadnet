Shade the roads you can see

You'll see satellite image tiles (5.8 m/pixel, false-colour). On each, paint over every road you can clearly see. Please spend about 20–30 minutes and do as many tiles as you can — you can stop and submit anytime.

1. Paint — press & drag over a road to shade it. Cover its full length and width.
2. Brush / Erase / Size — tune the brush; erase to fix mistakes.
3. Only what you're sure of — don't guess. No roads? tick No visible roads.
4. Each tile saves when you click Save & next. Click Finish when you're done.
